# RES2: mp_host_start.txt, then the HOST (one peer only) uses the map zoom while the lockstep match runs.
#
# The determinism claim is that the zoom, the crop origin and the camera tiles the zoom absorbs are view state:
# nothing hashed, nothing in the order pipeline or a packet. This script is what makes the claim a measurement --
# `test_ui.py --determinism --det-wheel` runs this on the host and the stock mp_client_start.txt on the client,
# and mp_analyze must still report ALL PAIRS IDENTICAL while the host's camera has been moved by the zoom's
# camera absorb (zooming out toward the edge of the viewport hands whole tiles to the camera) and the client's has not.
#
# Pair with mp_client_start.txt. The host's ini (tools/uiscripts/ini/map_zoom_det_host.ini) pins the owned
# DirectInput the `rawmouse` verb feeds.

settled value:110
capture m1_menu
clickv 110
present Player name
settled Ok
clickl Ok
settled Create
clickl Create
present Game name
settled Ok
clickl Ok
present Available maps
settled Ok
clickl Ok

present Network players
peers 1
awaitsignal lobby
enabled Start
settled Start
capture m2_lobby_ready
clickl Start

absent Network players
gameclock 6000
capture m3_launched
rawfocus 1

# zoom in at the right-hand side of the 864x736 viewport (the det run pins 1024x768), then out at the left edge: the out step needs tiles from the camera
cursorhold 800 300 30
rawmouse wheel 120
zoom ge 120
cursorhold 800 300 10
rawmouse wheel 120
cursorhold 800 300 10
rawmouse wheel 120
zoom ge 170
cursorhold 800 300 20
capture m4_zoomed_in
log HOST zoomed in 3 notches at the right edge
cursorhold 40 500 20
rawmouse wheel -120
cursorhold 40 500 10
rawmouse wheel -120
cursorhold 40 500 20
log HOST zoomed out 2 notches at the left edge (camera tiles absorbed)
# and back in, leaving the host zoomed while the match plays on
rawmouse wheel 120
cursorhold 400 300 10
rawmouse wheel 120
cursorhold 400 300 30
zoom ge 140
capture m5_zoomed_end
log HOST left the match zoomed -- the peers must still agree
# D15: and the wheel over the side-panel lists (the pump marks the current panel dirty, the draw wrapper scrolls the list under the
# pointer): notches back and forward over the panel at three heights, with the sim running between them. The panel's list scroll
# offsets and the refresh flag are view state; the peers must still agree.
cursorhold 904 300 10
rawmouse wheel -120
gameclock +200
cursorhold 904 300 10
rawmouse wheel -120
gameclock +200
cursorhold 904 500 10
rawmouse wheel 120
gameclock +200
cursorhold 904 500 10
rawmouse wheel -120
gameclock +200
cursorhold 400 300 10
rawmouse wheel 120
gameclock +200
cursorhold 904 300 10
rawmouse wheel 120
gameclock +200
capture m6_panel_wheeled
log HOST wheeled the side panel six times -- the peers must still agree
end
