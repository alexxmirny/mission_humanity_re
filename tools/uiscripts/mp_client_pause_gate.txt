# D19 MP GATE (client) -- the passive half. Joins the host, signals its lobby, enters the match and
# rides it long enough for the host's post-keypress sim assertion to be meaningful: in lockstep the
# host cannot advance past a step this peer has not reached, so the client must outlive the host's
# `simstep 600`.
#
# Same walk as mp_client_start.txt, minus its captures.
#
# THIS PEER IS WHY THE `simstep` PREDICATE EXISTS (fork F5J). A lockstep client's sim steps do NOT
# arrive on its own presents -- they arrive in bursts as the host's horizon is delivered -- so the
# step count at any clock threshold is set by the wire, and no capture-timing pin has purchase on it.
# Measured at F4H: with pin_wallclock on both peers the HOST capture came within one pixel and this
# one gave two distinct frames 3.5% apart (the ship and its ellipse one step out). `simstep` fences
# the sim AT the step instead, which is the only thing that reaches this case.
