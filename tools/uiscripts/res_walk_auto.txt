# ---- galaxy_native_cursor: entering and leaving the galaxy screen keeps the cursor where it is on the SCREEN.

# presented image rect to the screen and back through the new one (1920x1056 at (0,12)-(1920,1068), 1024x768 fitted in
# the 1920x1080 desktop). enter: (1200,300) -> (682,221); exit: the click on the DRAWN Back at (746,723) -> (1289,1005).
