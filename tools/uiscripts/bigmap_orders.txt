# bigmap_orders -- mods:MM4C, a screen_seq step (one process, libmh bound, 1024x768, ini/bigmap_orders.ini: [ui] bigmap_orders=1).
# 2-almost (64 x 64): unit 5 (a group of five insects, idle beside the base) is drag-selected, then Tab is held and the big map is
# right clicked. The side minimap's right click is what the overlay now does: the stock minimap ladder in llm_strat_input_update 0x00441b88:
#   a. an empty tile 22 tiles off the view's left edge: a deferred move; frames b_arrived after the unit walked there;
#   b. a tile in the unexplored dark: the same move ("tile not visible" arm);
#   c. control: the SAME tile as (a) clicked on the bare world (Tab released) -> the unit's goal is the same tile;
#   d. a double click on the overlay -> the confirmed move; e. Shift held -> the confirmed move;
#   f. an enemy building on the overlay -> an attack order (control: the same building clicked on the bare world, same goal);
#   h. a building armed for placement -> cancelled, no order (arming drops the unit selection, as in the stock game);
#   g. nothing selected -> no order.
# The verdict is tools/check_bigmap.py --orders over the `; BIGMAP: order at` lines and the `; [goal]` lines (loggoal), not a frame.
