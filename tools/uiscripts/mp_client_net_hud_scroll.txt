# segment 2 (TL-UISTALL): the host blackholes host -> client; `stalled` gates the capture on the SIM actually being frozen (>= 400 ms, the
# indicator's own STALL_SHOW_MS) rather than on a wall-clock guess. The host lifts the blackhole once this peer has captured.
