Downloads

One launcher. Your own copy of the game.

Place mh_launcher.exe beside mh.exe and run it. It installs and updates the project's DLLs. Do not run it as administrator; it asks when installation needs elevation.

The newest published launcher release by publication date, independent of game-only tags. It may be a prerelease; the stable channel is shown separately below.

What each channel offers

Stable is explicitly promoted by the maintainer. Latest follows release publication and may contain prereleases. A final-looking version or GitHub's “Latest” badge does not mean it has been promoted to stable.

@@CHANNELS@@

Channel entries come from manifests verified at site generation, not a live browser update check. The launcher performs its own signature, freshness and compatibility checks. Downloading a launcher here does not silently change your selected game channel.
