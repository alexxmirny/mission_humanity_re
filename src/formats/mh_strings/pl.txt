#
# Our own translation, not retail data: src/formats/langpack.py copies it into lang/<id>/ beside the
# pack it builds. One `key = text` per line, UTF-8; keys and the printf shape of every text (the
# %s %d %u %lu conversions, in order) must match src/mh_dll/mh/ui/player_strings.def, or mh.dll
# shows that key's English. tools/lint_player_strings.py and `langpack.py --selftest` check both.
# Plain "-" throughout (the menu fonts' em dash is not guaranteed). The Polish letters need the
# Extermination-derived fonts of the pl pack (RL18).
