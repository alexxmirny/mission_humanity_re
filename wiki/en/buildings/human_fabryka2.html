    Description

    This is a heavy industrial engineering complex based on nuclear technology that produces heavy combat units and aircraft. If expanded, the heavy factory can also produce light helicopters. Newly produced ground vehicles are placed in garages, planes at the airport and the helicopters on the helipad.
