    Description

    The Pioneer is the foundation stone of your colony, and your personal flagship. It can carry to a new planet all the elements needed to start a new colony (raw materials and people), and provides rudimentary living accommodation, power generation and storage when it arrives at its destination.

    The Pioneer can move around a single planet, or between planets, enabling various expansion strategies. The Pioneer has to be fully operational (100% energy) before it can launch. When it is airborne, its weaker underside is exposed, and the Pioneer is more prone to damage.

    Without the Pioneer present, no building on a planet can take place, and if it is destroyed, so are you, and your hopes to build a new civilisation.
