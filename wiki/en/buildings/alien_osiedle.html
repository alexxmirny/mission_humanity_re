    Description

    Expanding our colony requires a huge number of drones, and the drones have to live somewhere. The hive is such a place. Drones selected for warrior training are also taken from here. The population increase depends on the available space in the hives. Work on population growth is limited to drones not assigned to other tasks. If there is too little space for the current number of drones, natural selection, "the survival of the fittest" takes place until the number of drones is equal to the amount of living space. Being quite a stubborn race, with a fierce temper, this process can take place remarkably rapidly.
