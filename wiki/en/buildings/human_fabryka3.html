    Description

    This is the most advanced military vehicle factory. The Mega-factory utilises the newest technologies and production methods. After expansion it can produce the most powerful human military units: faster, stronger and more powerfully armed. Vehicles constructed here are placed in your garages, at the airport or the helipad.
