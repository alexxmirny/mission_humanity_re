    Description

    Gaining air supremacy is one of the best methods of winning the war - or so went the classic earth war doctrine. Battle conditions have changed since that time, but a good assault fighter is still worth a great deal. The Crusader project aims at creating a modern aircraft based on ion technology and capable of shooting down enemy planes as well as laying fire on ground targets. Anticipated weapons include the Humming-bird rockets and ion guns.
