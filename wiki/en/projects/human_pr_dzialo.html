    Description

    The necessity of testing our new ion weapons gave birth to the S.D.P. project. A tower with an ion gun was added to a heavy tank, resulting in the creation of a powerful, yet sluggish vehicle. Its firepower is astonishing, but the total weight of the heavy armour and still imperfect weaponry make it remarkably slow. With no room left for ground to air weaponry, support vehicles are recommended.
