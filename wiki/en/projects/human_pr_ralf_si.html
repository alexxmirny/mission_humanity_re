    Description

    The Alpha type rocket, despite all its drawbacks, had one advantage: it was cheap. This allowed for mass production of vehicles equipped with Alpha rockets. According to the rule that mass production leads to gains in manufacturing quality, the designers decided to improve the Alpha rocket without raising production costs. This project will allow for the creation of high-explosive charges increasing the accuracy of the rocket and the overall firepower of the vehicles equipped.
