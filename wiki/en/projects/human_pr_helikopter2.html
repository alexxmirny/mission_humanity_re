    Description

    The Atilla helicopter became outdated as a result of the development of enemy ground and air forces. Nestor is to be its successor--an armoured assault helicopter making use of, among others things, ion weapons. Despite its large mass, Nestor will be relatively fast and manoeuvrable. This kind of vehicle can be a decisive factor in battling the aliens.
