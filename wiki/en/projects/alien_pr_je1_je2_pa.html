    Description

    Light reconnaissance Cerberus class vehicles suffered greatly when aircraft were introduced because their reconnaissance role was taken over by planes. Cerberuses were then re-assigned to front line combat. They are not suited for this purpose, however, due to weak armour. Therefore, the purpose of this project is to equip these vehicles with more effective armour.
