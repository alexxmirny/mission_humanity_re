    Description

    Fighting aliens at close range means heavy loses. Therefore, the idea of creating long-range rocket artillery seems very appealing. The S.D.R. vehicle will be designed to fire rockets at an enemy base while staying beyond the range of even the most advanced alien patrols. This vehicle will require air support in case of enemy helicopter attack or fast reconnaissance units, because, as a cost-cutting measure, no weapons will be incorporated to protect against such attack.
