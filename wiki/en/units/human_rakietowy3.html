    Description

    This self-propelled rocket cannon is a modern version of long-range units utilized during terrestrial wars. Thanks to the use of both its original power source and an ion drive it can operate over an unlimited area. Equipped with ballistic Gamma class rockets, it is able to destroy targets beyond its own direct visible range. Extremely useful for picking off enemy forces discovered during reconnaissance, it is however completely defenceless against infantry or quick armoured enemy units. Requires constant protection from air and ground attack, or good camouflage in the field.
