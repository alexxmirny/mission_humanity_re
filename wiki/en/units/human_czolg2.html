    Description

    A heavy defence/attack tank equipped with a modern heavy 75mm cannon fitted with a stabilized barrel and active armour. This vehicle's additional equipment includes two turret mounted 14mm guns. The ammo used by the Scorpio is far ahead of its time. The active tungsten core projectiles can pierce through even the most advanced alien plasma armour. It is also equipped with a friend/foe identification system and an ion drive is required to move this heavy beast, even at a sedate pace.

    This ideal human armoured weapon will form the cornerstone of your attack and defence strategies. It is even more effective when supported by helicopters.
