    Description

    This light attack tank is based upon standard terrestrial fighting techniques. It is used as a support vehicle for ground infantry in direct combat. Due to its limited firing range, it is best suited for destroying enemy mobile units at point blank range. It is both light and manoeuvrable. Combat tested many times in terrestrial wars. Unfortunately, in the current war theatre it does not match up to more advanced enemy units. Due to our standardized production technology, it is perfectly suited for future conversion to more advance technologies. Equipped with a conventional propulsion system and classic armour. Armed with a standard self-loading 40mm gun and light cannon.
