    Description

    Further development of our rocket artillery gave birth to this Hydra model. The rocket in the Hydra model can be fired at enemy targets from a very great distance which eliminates the risk of enemy response. This vehicle, however, has neither strong armour nor speed and therefore has to be protected, especially against air attack.
