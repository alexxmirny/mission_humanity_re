    Description

    A heavy bomber designed mainly to attack enemy vehicle columns not protected by pill-boxes or towers. Equipped with wave demolition bombs, it is perfectly suited for sudden air raids on enemy targets. This flying giant is also fitted with needle guns for air target destruction, although this should be looked on as a defensive weapon.
