    Description

    Our breakthrough achievement, this is the pinnacle of human military design. This fast, manoeuvrable, and excellently armoured air vehicle is a real battle winner. Due to its high specification it is known as "the flying fortress". Its main tasks are to eliminate the enemy armoured military forces and long-range artillery, but it can be successfully used for enemy base attack. Its weapons include an ion gun, a 14mm gun and B22 class rockets.
