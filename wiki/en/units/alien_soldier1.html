    Description

    The Insect is a warrior whose only purpose is to kill and be killed. Insects constitute the core of alien invasion forces. They are armed with needle guns; they are mobile and difficult to hit. They also provide backup for combat vehicles. Insects can be grouped together which allows for more efficient management of these units.
