    Description

    Heavy, patrol/combat vehicle designed mainly for anti-aircraft defence. Equipped with a fully automatic 14mm gun and rocket launcher, it's characterised by high its fire power and efficiency. Designed mainly for air targets operating at low altitudes. In mobile war situations it is mainly used as a backup and escort for armoured units against fighter and helicopter attacks. Equipped with a conventional engine and weak armour, it definitely requires land backup in order to repel potential surface attacks.
