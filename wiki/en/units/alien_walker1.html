    Description

    Light mid-range walker. Standard military equipment. Difficult terrain conditions in our mother Solar system shaped its development. Modernised, this machine is equipped with identification and early detection systems and is in constant further development. It is a classic example of the direction of alien military research. A remarkably fast unit, it operates in difficult terrain and is mostly used to identify attacking enemy structures. It is equipped with plasma rockets and a needle gun and can escape enemy attack relatively easily thanks to excellent manoeuvrability and quick acceleration.
