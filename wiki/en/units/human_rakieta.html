    Description

    Small space shuttle equipped with long-range engines used for the transportation of raw materials and humans (housed in cryo-chambers) to distant planets. Due to the simplicity of its construction, it needs only a space station for both launching and landing. It can remain in orbit for some time if the space station on the target planet is inoperative.
