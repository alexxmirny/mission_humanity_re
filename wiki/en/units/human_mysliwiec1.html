    Description

    A supersonic light fighter designed almost exclusively for base defence against enemy aircraft and helicopter attacks. The production technology was taken from Earth, and is, therefore, a bit outdated. Due to the high cost of modernization, efforts to update this machine were abandoned and have been redirected towards the construction of a successor with the working name Crusader. In the preliminary stages of alien combat it is the only air force protection available for humans. Its weapons include a 14mm gun and Albatross type air-to-air rockets.
