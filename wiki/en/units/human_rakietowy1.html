    Description

    Mid-range rocket vehicle based on the light Anaconda tank chassis. Armed with standard, inaccurate, Alpha class surface-to-surface rockets. Utilized mainly for destroying large static objects and therefore usually takes part in the final enemy liquidation process. In extreme cases can also be used as a "fire wall" for attacking armoured and infantry units.
