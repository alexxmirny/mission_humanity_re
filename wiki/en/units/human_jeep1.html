    Description

    Light cross-country reconnaissance vehicle with a wide visual range and good speed under difficult conditions. Equipped with a standard 7mm assault gun. The main advantage of this jeep is its long range and velocity. Hence, its tasks are above all battlefield reconnaissance, locating enemy targets for other units to destroy. Whilst initially designed as a support vehicle for infantry units, its high speed allows it to be effective against un-supported lightly armoured enemy artillery.
