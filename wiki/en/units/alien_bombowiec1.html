    Description

    A light supersonic diving fighter designed mainly to destroy the enemy armoured columns and structures. It is armed with remote control, bundle suction bombs. It is not designed for direct combat with any type of armoured vehicles, and is practically doomed when used as a backup for its own fighter units. It most often operates in squads together with Harpy fighters, its natural protection.
