    Description

    Heavy long-range rocket vehicle designed for destroying ground and air targets. Utilizes a nuclear propulsion system. Equipped with a modern multi-purpose B22 rocket. It is characterised by low rocket launch speed and quite good firepower, but most importantly, it's amazing accuracy in target destruction. It can be used successfully as a backup for units with a short field of fire, as well as for direct attack on enemy units and structures. Requires support from land troops. It is practically defenceless against close range and air attacks.
