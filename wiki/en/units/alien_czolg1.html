    Description

    A Light tracked vehicle with plasma armour and modern cathode weapons. The Vandalus's main task is to wipe out attacking infantry units and provide backup for both its own infantry units and long range artillery. Modern technology, low weight and strong armour are its main advantages. According to the military doctrine established during the Earth conquest, Vandalus-type units should be the core of attacking divisions. Their high velocity and manoeuvrability allow for operation in practically all conditions.
